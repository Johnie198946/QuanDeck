## Goal and changes

Prevent a recovered or adopted external source with no verified process stop from becoming archived/cancelled in the project ledger. Persist the driver acknowledgement separately from the visible status. Project deletion and follow-up dispatch can inspect the acknowledgement without inferring that a local paused/archived record stopped the source.

Changed files:
- scripts/runtime/driver.mjs: trusted stoppedConfirmed event field; unverified stop stays paused and archive/cancel rejects.
- src/data/runtimeContracts.ts: receipt/event/session acknowledgement metadata.
- src/data/runtimeDataSource.ts: persist control acknowledgement, protect terminal control events, propagate adoption and recovery evidence, current snapshot wins over replay.
- scripts/runtime/driver.test.mjs and tests/runtime-data.test.ts: fresh close, restart, resumed work, external source, incomplete stop and stale event regressions.
